# Shared Mailbox and Distribution List Access

## Requesting access
1. Open the IT Self-Service portal and choose "Request access to shared mailbox".
2. Enter the shared mailbox address and the level of access needed (Read, Send As, Send on Behalf).
3. The mailbox owner must approve the request.

## After approval
Access is granted within 4 hours. Shared mailboxes appear automatically in Outlook after a restart. In Outlook on the web, right-click "Folders" > "Add shared folder or mailbox".

## Distribution lists
Owners can add or remove members themselves in Outlook > Address Book. For new distribution lists, raise a ticket.
