# Password Reset
Category: Access

## When to use
You forgot your password, or your password has expired.

## Self-service reset
1. Go to the self-service password portal at `https://passwordreset.corp.example.com`.
2. Enter your corporate email address and complete MFA verification.
3. Choose a new password: minimum 14 characters, including upper case, lower case, a number and a symbol. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.
4. On a corporate laptop, lock and unlock the screen (Windows + L) while connected to VPN so the new password syncs.

## Password expiry
Passwords expire every 90 days. You receive reminder emails 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

## Escalation
If self-service reset fails, contact the Service Desk by phone; agents can reset it after verifying your identity.
