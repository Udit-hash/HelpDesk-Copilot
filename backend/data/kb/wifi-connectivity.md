# Office Wi-Fi Problems

## Networks
- `CORP-SECURE`: for corporate laptops; connects automatically using your device certificate.
- `CORP-GUEST`: for visitors and personal devices; requires daily registration.

## Resolution steps
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
2. Forget the `CORP-SECURE` network and reconnect.
3. If the laptop has not been on the office network for over 60 days, its certificate may have expired: connect with a LAN cable once and restart.
4. Check whether colleagues near you have the same problem. If yes, it may be an access point outage.

## Escalation
raise a ticket with your building, floor and the approximate location. Mark it High if a whole floor is affected.
