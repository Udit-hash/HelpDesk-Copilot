# New Joiner Accounts and Access

## Before day one
The hiring manager raises a "New Joiner" request at least 5 working days before the start date. This creates the account, mailbox and laptop order.

## Day one
1. Collect your laptop from the IT desk on your floor.
2. Sign in with the temporary password sent to your manager, then change it.
3. Set up MFA (see the MFA Setup article).
4. Install the apps you need from the Software Center.

## Additional access
Access to applications, shared drives and mailboxes is requested through the IT Self-Service portal and approved by the resource owner.
